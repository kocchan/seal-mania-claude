<h2>Contents of “Code Geass: Lelouch of the Rebellion Mark Accessories”</h2>
<p>From the 2006 anime series “Code Geass: Lelouch of the Rebellion,” “Marker Accessories” featuring cute, stylized versions of the characters are now available! These pre-painted mascots stand approximately 3 cm tall and come with a crab clasp and ring parts.</p>
<p>In-store release is scheduled for December 2026. Pre-orders will begin on Gashapon Online on Tuesday, August 25, 2026, at 11:00 AM, with each play priced at 400 yen (tax included).</p>

<h2>Pre-order and Sales Information on Gashapon Online</h2>
<p>Pre-orders on Gashapon Online will begin on Tuesday, August 25, 2026, at 11:00 AM, with shipping scheduled for around late December 2026. Additionally, orders for “Batch 2: Shipping in January 2027” are being accepted; the deadline for this batch is Thursday, September 24, 2026, at 11:59 PM.</p>
<p>Please note that orders for both batches will close once the allocated stock is exhausted. Shipping costs are a flat rate of 660 yen (tax included) nationwide, and it has been announced that the items may be sold at Gashapon vending machines across the country prior to delivery.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions About “Code Geass: Lelouch of the Rebellion Marker Accessories”</h2>
<h3>When will the “Marker Accessories” be released?</h3>
<p>In-store release is scheduled for December 2026. Pre-orders will begin on Gashapon Online on Tuesday, August 25, 2026, at 11:00 AM, with shipping scheduled for around late December 2026; the “Second Batch: January 2027 Shipping” is scheduled for around late January 2027.</p>
<h3>How much do they cost?</h3>
<p>400 yen per play (tax included). Shipping costs for purchases made on Gashapon Online are a flat rate of 660 yen (tax included) nationwide.</p>
<h3>How many types are in the lineup?</h3>
<p>There are 5 types in total: Lelouch, C.C., Suzaku, Kallen, and Lelouch (20th Anniversary Costume Ver.). The win rate for each is listed as 20.0% on Gashapon Online.</p>
<h3>What are the size and materials?</h3>
<p>The figures are approximately 3 cm in size. The pre-painted mascots are made of PVC, and the included parts are made of silicone. The recommended age is 15 and up.</p>
<h3>Can I buy them at Gashapon vending machines (in stores)?</h3>
<p>The official announcement states that they may be sold at Gashapon vending machines nationwide prior to online delivery. You can check the list of participating stores on the official website.</p>
