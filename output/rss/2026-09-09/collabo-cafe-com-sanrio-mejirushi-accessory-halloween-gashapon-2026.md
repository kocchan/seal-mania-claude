<h2>Details on Sanrio’s “Marker Accessories: Halloween!”</h2>
<p>New Halloween-themed items are being added to Sanrio Characters’ popular “Marker Accessories” series.</p>
<p>These items can be attached to the handles of bags and pouches to serve as markers for your belongings!</p>
