<h2>Sutopuri Bookmark Accessory Lineup</h2>
<p>Scheduled to go on sale at capsule vending machines nationwide starting in November 2026, these products are bookmark accessories modeled after the members of “Sutopuri.”</p>
<p>The hooded members are rendered in 3D in their respective member colors, with a total of 5 varieties in the lineup!</p>

<h3>Featuring clear molding and a crown charm</h3>
<p>Each figure is molded from translucent parts in the members’ respective colors, with even the ribbons around their necks matching their colors.</p>
<p>A separate crown-shaped charm—based on the motif featured in the Sutopuri logo—is included, and only Riku is depicted wearing a hood with cat ears!</p>

<h2>Stupri Landmark Accessories: Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3>When will they be released?</h3>
<p>They are scheduled for release in November 2026, though the exact date has not been announced. Release dates may vary by region and store.</p>
<h3>Where can I buy them?</h3>
<p>They will be sold at capsule vending machines nationwide.</p>
<h3>How much does one play cost?</h3>
<p>400 yen (tax included).</p>
<h3>How many designs are in the lineup?</h3>
<p>There are 5 designs in total: Riken, Satomi, Ruuto, Gel, and Koron.</p>
<h3>What do the designs look like?</h3>
<p>The designs feature three-dimensional, clear-molded figures of the members wearing hoods in their respective member colors, each with a crown-motif charm. Only Riken is depicted wearing a hood with cat ears.</p>
<h3>Are there products for other groups?</h3>
<p>It has been confirmed that “Knight X,” “AMPTAKxCOLORS,” “Meteorites,” “SneakerStep,” and “True&amp;Lip” will also have their “Mejiurushi” accessories released.</p>
