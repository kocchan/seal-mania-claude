<h2>The lineup for Dokomus Channel’s “Doko Usa Mark Charms”</h2>
<p>The “Doko Usa Mark Charms” will be released sequentially at capsule toy vending machines nationwide starting in early September 2026.</p>
<p>While retaining the familiar round shape of the channel icon, five different designs—including a devil look and pixel art—are now available as charms!</p>

<h2>Docoms Channel “Doko Usame Jirushi Charm” Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3>When will the “Doko Usame Mark Charm” go on sale?</h3>
<p>It will be released sequentially at capsule toy vending machines nationwide starting in early September 2026. The exact release date may vary by store.</p>
<h3>Where can I buy them?</h3>
<p>They are scheduled to be sold at capsule toy counters nationwide, primarily at “gashacoco” capsule toy shops.</p>
<h3>What is the price and how many varieties are available?</h3>
<p>The price is 400 yen (tax included), and each capsule contains one charm. There are five designs in total: Devil Doko-Usa, Peace, Weird Coms Channel, Fake, and Dot Doko-Usa.</p>
<h3>Can I choose the design?</h3>
<p>Since the items are distributed randomly, you cannot choose the design. Even if you purchase multiple capsules, you may receive the same design.</p>
