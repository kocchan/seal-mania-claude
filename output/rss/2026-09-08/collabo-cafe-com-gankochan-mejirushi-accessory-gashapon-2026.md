<h2 id="cc-sakuhin">Product Information for “Ganko-chan from the Buzzing Forest” “Landmark Accessories”</h2>
<p>“Ganko-chan from the Buzzing Forest” is a moral education program in the form of a puppet show broadcast on NHK E-Tele.</p>
<p>Set on Earth in the distant future after the extinction of humanity, the series depicts the daily life of Ganko-chan—a child of a new generation of dinosaurs—as she spends her days with her classmates at Mannaka Elementary School in the Buzzing Forest.</p>
<p>The series began airing in 1996 and is a long-running program that has been used as a teaching resource for ethics classes in many elementary schools.</p>
